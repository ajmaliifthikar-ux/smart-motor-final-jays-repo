## 2024-05-14 - Prevent repetitive Intl instantiations in formatting and loops
**Learning:** Instantiating `Intl` objects (`Intl.NumberFormat`, `Intl.DateTimeFormat`) is computationally expensive and causes parsing overhead. Inside frequently called functions (like `formatPrice` or `formatAED`) or render loops, this can cause significant memory pressure and unnecessary re-renders or sluggish performance. Also, using `Intl.DateTimeFormat().resolvedOptions().timeZone` just to pass it to `toLocaleDateString()` is redundant because `toLocaleDateString()` defaults to the local timezone natively.
**Action:** Always hoist and cache `Intl` instances at the module scope when formatting numbers and dates, especially when rendering arrays or generating invoices. Avoid redundant `Intl` instantiation just to retrieve local timezone when `toLocaleDateString()` natively defaults to it.
